# CARGO HOLD - Lore Bible

## The Setting: Post-Silence Era

Three centuries ago, humanity spanned thousands of worlds connected by the **Beacon Network**—ancient structures left by the **Priors**, a civilization that vanished millions of years before humans left Earth. The Beacons allowed instant communication and navigation across the void.

Then came **the Silence**. In a single day, every Beacon went dark. The network collapsed. Billions died in the chaos—ships lost in transit, stations cut off from supply, wars over dwindling resources. Entire sectors went dark.

Now, 300 years later, humanity survives in isolated clusters. **Jump drives** allow travel between stars, but without the Beacons, navigation is dangerous. Ships disappear. The void between stars is full of debris, derelicts, and things that prey on the desperate.

## The Priors

The Priors built the Beacon Network and then vanished—millions of years before humanity existed. Their artifacts are rare, valuable, and poorly understood. The Science Collective studies them obsessively.

Key Prior artifacts:
- **Beacons**: The network nodes, now dead. Some still hum with residual energy.
- **Memory Cores**: Crystalline data storage containing archives from before the Silence.
- **The Void Compass**: Points somewhere. Always the same direction. No one knows where.

What killed the Priors? No one knows. The Science Collective has theories. None are comforting.

## The Silence

Nobody knows what caused the Silence. Theories include:
- Prior technology finally failing after millions of years
- Something waking up that shouldn't have
- A deliberate shutdown by unknown parties
- A cascade failure triggered by human interference

The truth may be in the data cores. Or it may be something better left unknown.

## The Factions

### Free Traders Guild
The backbone of inter-system commerce. They kept what was left of civilization connected after the Silence. Pragmatic, profit-driven, but with a code: cargo gets delivered, contracts get honored, and Guild members help each other in the void.

**Reputation effects**: Better prices at Guild ports, access to premium contracts, emergency assistance.

### Frontier Alliance
Colonists on the edge of known space. Fiercely independent, always in need, suspicious of the core worlds that abandoned them during the Silence. They remember who helped and who didn't.

**Reputation effects**: Access to frontier resources, salvage rights, colony defense contracts.

### Industrial Consortium
Controls the forges and foundries that keep ships flying. Credits talk, everything else walks. They don't care about your politics or your past—only whether you can pay and deliver.

**Reputation effects**: Discounts on ship repairs and modules, bulk ore contracts, manufacturing access.

### Science Collective
Obsessed with understanding the Priors and the Silence. They hoard knowledge, pay fortunes for artifacts, and share nothing. What they've learned in three centuries of study, they keep to themselves.

**Reputation effects**: Access to Prior artifacts, advanced technology, research contracts.

## Your Ship: The Holdfast

A light freighter—nothing special, but she's yours. Inherited, won, or stolen (you don't talk about it). What matters is she flies, and the hold is empty.

The Holdfast has seen better days. The previous owner left their mark in a thousand small ways: scratches on the bulkheads, a lucky charm welded to the console, logs you haven't had the heart to delete. She's home now.

## The Void

Space between stars is not empty. It's full of:
- **Derelicts**: Ships lost in the Silence or since. Some are tombs. Some are salvage. Some are traps.
- **Pirates**: Desperate people doing desperate things. Some were honest haulers once.
- **Debris fields**: Shattered stations, dead fleets, the wreckage of the old network.
- **Anomalies**: Things that don't fit known physics. The Priors left more than Beacons.
- **The dark**: Sectors that went silent and never came back. Ships that enter don't return.

## Technology

### Jump Drives
Humanity's own invention, developed centuries before contact with Prior technology. Allows faster-than-light travel, but requires precise navigation. Without the Beacons, every jump is a calculated risk.

### Prior Tech
Poorly understood, impossible to replicate, incredibly valuable. Memory cores, navigation aids, materials that shouldn't exist. The Science Collective pays well because they can't make more.

### The Network (Dead)
The Beacon Network once allowed instant communication across the galaxy. Now the Beacons are dark monuments. Some still hum. Some contain data. All are considered dangerous to approach.

## Themes

- **Survival**: Resources are scarce. Every choice has a cost.
- **Legacy**: The past haunts everything. The Priors. The Silence. Your own history.
- **Connection**: In the void, relationships matter. Crew, contacts, reputation.
- **Mystery**: The universe is full of unanswered questions. Some answers are worth seeking. Others are worth avoiding.
- **Work**: Honest or otherwise, cargo needs moving. That's the job. That's survival.

## Tone

Cold. Sparse. Moments of beauty in the dark. Gallows humor. Hope as a survival mechanism. The universe doesn't care about you, but the people in it might.

Not grimdark—there's light in the void. But it's hard-won and easily lost.
